            Willits Local

            A free neighborhood directory for Willits, California: ZIP code 95490, from Brooktrails to Ridgewood Ranch.

            Why are some places outside the city limits? ZIP 95490 is far bigger than the city. It takes in Brooktrails, Ridgewood Ranch and Howard Forest, Sherwood Valley, Longvale and Hearst.

            Willits grew up in the Little Lake Valley, where Hiram Willits settled in 1857. The town took his name in 1874 and incorporated in 1888. The Skunk Train’s line through the redwoods ends at its 1915 depot, an arch from Reno has spanned Main Street since 1995, and Willits Frontier Days, held since 1927, celebrated its 100th year in 2026.
