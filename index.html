            Fort Bragg Local

            A free neighborhood directory for Fort Bragg, California: ZIP code 95437, from Cleone to the Noyo River.

            Why are some places outside the city limits? ZIP 95437 is bigger than the city. It takes in Cleone and MacKerricher to the north, Noyo Harbor, the Highway 20 corridor toward Willits and Highway 1 south past the Botanical Gardens.

            Fort Bragg began in 1857 as an Army post on the Mendocino Indian Reservation, named for Braxton Bragg. In 1885 lumbermen arrived and laid the redwood railroad now known as the Skunk Train, and the town’s big mill ran until 2002. Today about 7,000 neighbors live between Noyo Harbor’s fishing boats, the Coastal Trail and Glass Beach.
